# Portrait copyright and reuse audit

Checked: 3 November 2026. Scope: 37 people in the survey. Logos, book covers and survey text are outside this portrait audit.

The page displays all 37 portraits: 28 have recorded reuse grants or author releases; 9 have unconfirmed reuse permission and were restored at the user’s explicit request on 3 November 2026. Displaying those 9 does not establish copyright permission. This is a source-based license check, not a claim that all rights are cleared in every jurisdiction. No direct permissions were requested or obtained.

## Current display decision

The user explicitly requested the portraits whose reuse rights remain unconfirmed. Restored: Allen Newell, Zenon Pylyshyn, Dedre Gentner, Charles Spearman, José Hernández-Orallo, Yi Ma, David Silver, David Ha and Brenden Lake. Attribution is included for each source; their audit status and license findings are unchanged. No direct rights-holder permission has been obtained for them.

## Attribution and retained files

The page includes creator credits, source links, image license links and display-change notices. Turing’s NoDerivatives image is displayed in full, with proportional resizing only. Noncommercial-only licenses are used on the user’s explicitly confirmed noncommercial basis. ShareAlike applies to adaptations of the relevant images. Original colors are preserved. Institution or subject names alone do not establish who owns a photograph. A source being online, educational use, small size or attribution alone does not supply permission.

The 9 unconfirmed files are now referenced by both the page and the interactive taxonomy at the user’s explicit request. Their footer entries state that reuse permission is unconfirmed. They must not be described as cleared merely because the page is noncommercial. The JSON audit records SHA-256 checksums and previous_versions for replaced sources; previous versions are historical evidence, not current display permissions.

Identity checks excluded images of a different David Silver, an unrelated Spearman and a gravestone. All initials have now been replaced by the existing source portraits. The unresolved permission findings remain unchanged.
