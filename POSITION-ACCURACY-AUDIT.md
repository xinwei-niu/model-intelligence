# Position-assignment accuracy audit

Checked: 3 November 2026. All 37 people and 41 person–position assignments were reviewed against main.tex and the cited primary works. The page’s static captions, tree previews and interactive details were synchronized. The manuscript was not modified.

The taxonomy groups related proposals and disagreements. These assignments identify contributions in particular works, not exclusive memberships, whole-career beliefs, or proof that a theory of intelligence is correct. The four research questions and eight programs remain those of the manuscript; Brooks is an adjacent implementation stance, not a ninth program.

## Corrections and qualifications

- Hawkins: identify Thousand Brains as a parallel-model alternative discussed alongside predictive processing.
- Gardner: identify multiple intelligences as a pluralist critique of standard psychometrics; broaden the subsection title accordingly.
- Putnam: scope functionalism/multiple realizability to the historical account.
- Lake: identify the 2023 implementation as neural meta-learning with Baroni, distinct from Marcus’s hybrid symbolic proposal.
- Hutter: distinguish the universal intelligence measure from the AIXI agent framework.
- Silver and Sutton: label their shared reward-is-enough claim as a hypothesis.
- Schmidhuber: use compression progress as the curiosity signal, not compressibility alone.
- Ma: specify maximal coding rate reduction and its representation-learning scope.
- Chomsky: specify constraints on language learning.
- Bender: credit the form–meaning argument with Koller and retain its scope.
- Searle, Harnad, Bender, Dreyfus and Brooks: preserve their different objections and engineering claims; do not collapse them into blanket opposition to machine intelligence.

## Verification limits

Some original publisher endpoints returned access errors. In those cases, the check used the same primary work hosted by its author/coauthor or a university, the publisher’s indexed abstract, or the author’s own summary. No classification rests on a secondary biography alone. Existing selected-reading links remain in the relevant page sections. The JSON ledger records each manuscript citation key, exact assignment and source.

The review checks attribution and defensible placement. It does not adjudicate whether predictive processing, symbolic architectures, multiple intelligences, the reward hypothesis or other disputed positions are true.
